Why We Built Saramaga

Music should be accessible to everyone — not locked behind subscriptions or interrupted by ads. Saramaga was built for developers creating streaming apps, discovery engines, playlist explorers, and experimental music platforms.

This is infrastructure for builders. Clean. Structured. Free to use. Music without friction.
